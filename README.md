### Who are we?

Are you a PhD researcher exploring a topic related to the circular economy at a Dutch university? Would you like to help nurture and grow an exciting circular economy network by participating in networking events, research talks, and excursions? Then we invite you to join the **Dutch CE PhD Network**.

The network is part of the **[Dutch Academic Network on Circular Economy (DAN-CE)](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/towards-a-circular-economy-and-society/people-and-network/network-and-partners)**. It connects PhD candidates from Dutch universities conducting research on the circular economy — including Leiden University, TU Delft, Erasmus University Rotterdam, Utrecht University, Maastricht University, and others.

The network is an initiative supported by the **Leiden-Delft-Erasmus Centre for Sustainability (LDE-CfS)**, **Utrecht University**, and **Maastricht University**.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### What can you expect?

- 🤝 Access to a growing network of peers passionate about circular economy research
- 💬 A space to share knowledge, ideas, and experiences from your PhD work
- 🔗 Opportunities to explore synergies and future collaborations
- 🌍 Broader perspectives through exchange with researchers from very different disciplinary backgrounds

## 🗓 Upcoming event

<table>
<tr><td>

**Circular Society Day** — organised by the PhD & Postdoc Council of DAN-CE

📍 Playground Utrecht University, Vening Meineszgebouw C — [Princetonlaan 6, 3584 CB Utrecht](https://www.google.nl/maps/place/Princetonlaan+6,+3508+TA+Utrecht)
📅 26 November · Sign-up opens 14 September
🎗 Supported by the UU Youth Fund and the ACT! consortium

A full-day event bringing together PhD and postdoc researchers from all disciplines working on the transition to a circular society in the Netherlands. Living within planetary boundaries while ensuring wellbeing requires real system transformation — across academic disciplines and beyond. This event is a first step toward finding convergence, leverage points, and tensions across the field.

**Programme**
- **Plenary panel** — researchers, policymakers, and NGO representatives on collaboration across academic and non-academic "knowledge silos", with audience Q&A
- **Pitches & presentations** — participants pitch (10 min incl. discussion) or present (20 min incl. discussion) ongoing or recent work
- **Afternoon workshop**, hosted by [Community Sense](https://www.communitysense.nl/) — mapping the participant network across academic dimensions, identifying gaps, and co-creating "seeds of change" for an early-career research action agenda in circular society research

*Registration form and full agenda to follow — check back soon, or watch our [LinkedIn group](#).*
