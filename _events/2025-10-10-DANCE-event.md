Don't miss the **8th DAN-CE event** this coming Friday October 10th.

During the conference the Dutch CE PhD network is hosting a session called “Challenges and opportunities for CE research in a multi-stakeholder and transdisciplinary context”. The session will give early career researchers the opportunity to connect with experienced scientists and practitioners from the field to learn from their experiences and together we will talk about how we can overcome common barriers in multi-stakeholder and transdisciplinary contexts.
